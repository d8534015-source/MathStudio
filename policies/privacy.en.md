## 1. Scope and responsibility
This draft describes MathStudio's tutoring website, OWNER service, teacher application, lesson tools and recording resources. The operator must confirm applicable privacy laws, purposes, service providers and guardian procedures. This document does not certify compliance or establish a student's legal capacity to consent.

## 2. Information used
Accounts contain a username, display name, password verifier and account role. Passwords are hashed; plaintext passwords are not retained by the application. Booking records associate accounts with lesson times, course choices and enrollment. Communications, uploaded resources, share-code grants, attendance and teacher absence-verification decisions are stored to support tutoring. The operator may retain guardian identity/evidence separately; the software stores account-linked scope, verification-reference digest, approval/expiry/withdrawal state and timestamps. Do not submit unnecessary identity documents or sensitive details in ordinary messages.

## 3. Live lessons
Enabling a camera, microphone or screen share sends available media through the meeting's WebRTC connections, using configured relay infrastructure when needed. Whiteboards and annotations are synchronized through OWNER. Sharing a screen may reveal other visible applications; close private material first. A disabled device is not intentionally included in the recorder. Connectivity failures may prevent remote media receipt. The application does not promise that every feed was received or recorded.

## 4. Recording choices and permission
Recording is disabled until OWNER approves a policy. Only teachers initiate/control recording. The software conservatively requires verified guardian authorization for every student and that student's meaningful agreement; OWNER must independently verify guardian identity and authority. A student's assertion of parental permission is insufficient. Optional standing authorization is separate from account Terms/Privacy acknowledgments. It identifies permitted storage (local/cloud), lesson-review purpose, retention, absent-enrollee audience and download permission. Where it validly covers a lesson, a visible notice replaces repetitive prompts; insufficient, changed, expired or withdrawn authorization blocks capture or requires fresh agreement. Guests without verified authorization cannot be recorded. Everyone receives a visible recording/paused notice; membership changes and withdrawal stop capture subject to bounded network/lease propagation.

## 5. Content and storage
Recordings may include enabled, received cameras/microphones, active shared screens, the board and synchronized teacher/student annotations. Cloud mode streams bounded chunks to private OWNER storage and private Cloudflare R2. Local mode uses a supported browser's chosen file location and does not automatically upload media; OWNER still receives authorization, duration and non-content integrity-checkpoint metadata. A later cloud upload requires explicit teacher action, matching integrity checkpoints and prior authorization covering cloud storage. Local save/crash failures can lose unfinished data. Teachers must secure their devices and follow agreed local retention/deletion procedures; the server cannot remotely erase local files.

## 6. Access and absent-student resources
The creating teacher manages the private recording library. A teacher may explicitly grant an authenticated, named account access only if it was enrolled in that scheduled lesson and is reliably verified absent. Uncertain attendance requires teacher verification; quick meetings do not acquire this eligibility. Sharing must be covered by the recorded participants' applicable guardian/student authorization. Codes are not public access credentials on their own. Playback and each Range/download request recheck access. Downloads are optional and may be prohibited. Do not redistribute recordings or disclose other students' information. Technical download controls cannot prevent every screen capture or copying after lawful access.

## 7. Withdrawal, deletion and retention
Account privacy controls withdraw standing authorization and revoke further sharing of that student's recorded participation. Participants can withdraw during a lesson. Guardian withdrawal is processed by OWNER. Already captured material is handled under the approved policy and applicable obligations; withdrawal does not itself erase all earlier recordings. Contact the privacy lead for a deletion/access request or dispute. Already downloaded copies cannot reliably be recalled. OWNER chooses cloud recording retention from 1–30 days and a retained-storage quota, and must configure private-bucket lifecycle cleanup. Expired media is denied access; network outages can delay physical deletion. Accounts, bookings and security/access audit metadata have separate schedules: [OWNER TO SUPPLY actual retention and backup deletion schedules]. Audit records contain account IDs, event type and timestamps, not video or message contents.

## 9. Authentication, requests and changes
Essential cookies support sessions and CSRF protection; the CSRF token is kept in server state and delivered to the page, not a separate CSRF cookie. Optional student Remember Me uses a rotated, revocable HttpOnly cookie (30-day absolute/7-day idle maximum). Language preference uses localStorage. The separate teacher application uses Windows DPAPI for its optional credentials. See the Cookie Policy for limitations. Ask the privacy lead about access/correction, account deletion, consent, guardian arrangements or complaints; identity verification and lawful exceptions may apply. Policy versions/acknowledgment times are recorded. Material recording-scope changes require fresh separate authorization; account acceptance never authorizes all future recordings.
